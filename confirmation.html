Important Information & Cautions

Before You Rent

  • Carry your valid Driving License and Government ID (Aadhaar/Passport)
  • Read rental agreements, insurance details, and payment terms carefully
  • Inspect vehicle for dents, scratches, and check tires, brakes, lights
  • Confirm vehicle insurance coverage

While Renting & Driving

  • Take video/photos of vehicle condition before starting
  • Do a short test run (100-200m) to check all controls
  • Always use provided helmets for two-wheelers
  • Be aware of local traffic regulations
  • Avoid smoking and keep interiors clean
  • Drive responsibly and within speed limits

Post-Rental

  • Return vehicle on time to avoid late fees
  • Ensure no new damage before deposit return
  • Return with same fuel level as provided
  • Report any issues immediately
